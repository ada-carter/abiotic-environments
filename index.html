Ultrasonic Transmission

bifurcation.jl
xₙ₊₁ = r·xₙ·(1−xₙ) + ε   H = −∑p·ln p
Logistic map iterated 2000× with noise injection. Shannon entropy H of the resulting distribution controls downstream amplitude shaping.

flayer.jl
f = f₀·2ᵒ·rᵢ   v += sin(φᵢ)·p   φᵢ += 2π·fᵢ·dt
Builds a frequency table spanning 9 octaves from equal-tempered and just-intonation ratios. Tones above a probability threshold p > 0.4 are additively synthesized. A coupling matrix diffuses energy between nearby partials.

dissonance.jl
d = |x″| / (1+|x′|)   H = ∑|x[i/k]|/k
Computes a curvature-weighted dissonance index from the second derivative of the audio signal. A harmonic ratio H derived from subharmonic indexing is blended with a running power envelope to produce the final dissonance mask.

interference.jl
v = (1+0.5·sin(2πμ))·b·sin(φ+πμ/4) → soft clip at ଐ.8
AM modulates the baseband signal onto a carrier whose frequency and phase shift are driven by the dissonance residual μ. A 64-tap exponentially-decaying convolution kernel adds acoustic diffusion. Output is soft-clipped to avoid hard saturation.
